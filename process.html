Process

A controlled path from material inspection to traceable finished product.

The supplied process flow connects raw-material inspection, drying, process setup, extrusion/co-extrusion, forming, trimming, thickness checks, final inspection, corrective action, packing and documentation.

01

Raw Material Inspection

Review the material documentation and basic conditions, including COA, material type and moisture.

02

Material Drying

Dry the applicable barrier materials, including EVOH / PA, before processing.

03

Process Setup

Set temperatures, speeds and layer ratios for the intended production process.

04

Extrusion & Co-Extrusion

Form the multi-layer structure through the extrusion / co-extrusion die.

05

Forming & Cooling

Shape the container and cool it through the applicable blow, cast or cooling operation.

06

Trimming & Thickness Check

Trim the formed container, verify thickness against tolerance, and adjust/recheck when required.

07

Final Product Inspection

Confirm the finished product meets the applicable requirements before acceptance or rejection.

08

Pack, Store & Trace

Accepted product is packed and stored, with batch, process parameters and test results documented for traceability.

Corrective action

When a result is not acceptable, the flow returns to the cause.

The source process includes rejection, root-cause analysis and corrective & preventive action (CAPA), followed by rechecking and documentation.

Traceability

Batch • Parameters • Test Results

Documentation and traceability close the production loop and connect the finished product to its process records.

4-Layer COEX Technology. Integrated IML.

MISR Company combines four-layer co-extrusion with In-Mold Labeling, integrating EVOH or PA barrier layers, tie layers, and HDPE structure into a single high-performance packaging solution.

Multi-Layer Barrier

Controlled layers create a packaging system with distinct functions.

The source describes co-extruded multi-layer bottles with precise thickness control for each layer, providing protection against contamination or damage while supporting lightweight industrial packaging.

01

EVOH / PA — Inner Barrier

The inner EVOH / PA layer provides the functional barrier within the packaging structure.

02

Tie Layer

The tie layer bonds the functional barrier layer with the surrounding polymer layers within the co-extruded structure.

03

Structural Layers

The structural layers form the main body of the packaging and provide the required strength and dimensional stability.

04

HDPE Layer

HDPE provides the structural polymer base of the packaging, supporting durability and overall container performance.
